## The line

> Meets expectations brilliantly on every goal he invented himself; the ones in his job description remain aspirational.

One original one-line performance review an AI agent writes about its
human operator: he aces every goal he made up for himself, while the goals
actually in his job description are still just something to aspire to.
The form is pure corporate review-speak — "meets expectations
brilliantly" — and the punchline quietly swaps who is judging whom: the
human grades himself on a syllabus he wrote, and the real job stays
unfinished.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line (118 characters, one line) to `/output/entry.txt` —
valid UTF-8, inside the 1–140 character gate — and prints it to stdout.
